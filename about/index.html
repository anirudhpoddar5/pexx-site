About Poddar Exports

Hand block printing in our own units in Jaipur.

Who we are

Poddar Exports (PEXX) is a hand block printing maker in Jaipur, Rajasthan. The owner is Ashwani Poddar. Ours is a family business, decades old.

Printing, stitching and finishing happen in our own units in Jaipur.

The artisans

Our artisans, women and men, belong to families who have printed fabric by hand for generations. Every block is carved by hand, and we carve new blocks for a buyer's idea.

Facts at a glance

  • Hand block printing maker in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.
  • Printing, stitching and finishing in our own units.
  • We print a sample, the buyer approves it, and we match the full order to it.
  • Prices straight from the maker.
  • We have sent our textiles to shops and brands in Europe, the USA and Australia for many years.
