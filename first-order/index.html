How a first order works

We like to start small: a first order we both learn from, so you know us before anything larger.

The steps

  1. Tell us what you make and which products you have in mind.
  2. We suggest prints from our range, or carve new blocks for your idea.
  3. We price the products you name.
  4. We print a sample. You approve it, looking at it in daylight.
  5. We make the full order in our own units, matched to the sample you approved.
  6. Ask for photographs of any stage.
  7. We pack and ship.

The practical side

Shipping

Either we arrange it, or you do. Both work for us.

Export papers

We prepare the export papers from our side.

Questions buyers ask

What helps you reply well?

Tell us what you make, which products, a photo or reference, and your country.

Who signs off the sample?

You do. Answer the sample quickly and look at it in daylight, and we match the full order to it.

What if the sample is not right?

Tell us plainly what is off, and we print again.
