Colour and handfeel

We block print with a soft handfeel. Colour fastness in washing is something we protect, and some of our buyers have our fabric tested at the best independent labs.

Your colour, matched

We print a sample, you approve it, and we match the full order to it. Every block is carved by hand, and we carve new blocks for your idea.

In our own units

Printing, stitching and finishing happen in our own units in Jaipur, so delivery is in our own hands. You are welcome to visit, or to ask for photographs of any stage.

From the maker

Prices straight from the maker. We have sent our textiles to shops and brands in Europe, the USA and Australia for many years.

Printed by hand, by families who know the work

Our artisans, women and men, belong to families who have printed fabric by hand for generations.

Visit Us & Print Workshops

Experience the craft of hand block printing through guided workshops held in our printing unit in Jaipur. These sessions offer an opportunity to engage closely with materials, tools and techniques that artisan families have used for generations.

Workshops are designed for individuals, small groups, and design-led visits seeking a deeper understanding of the process.
