Trade questions, answered

Short answers for shops, brands, importers and hotels buying from a maker in Jaipur.

These are the questions buyers ask us most. For anything else, write to us.

Questions buyers ask

Buying

Do you sell to small shops?

Yes. We like to start small: a first order we both learn from, so you know us before anything larger. More on wholesale.

How are your prices set?

Prices straight from the maker. They depend on the print, the base fabric and the colours, so tell us which products you want priced.

Which countries do you send to?

We have sent our textiles to shops and brands in Europe, the USA and Australia for many years.

How does a first order work?

See How a first order works.

Colour and quality

Can you match our colour?

Yes. We print a sample, you approve it, and we match the full order to it. Send a colour or a photo to start.

Do the colours run in the wash?

Colour fastness in washing is something we protect, and some of our buyers have our fabric tested at the best independent labs.

How does the fabric feel?

We block print with a soft handfeel, even where the print covers the whole piece.

Your own print

Do you sell fabric by the metre?

Yes. We can supply printed fabric by the metre. More on your own print.

Can you print our own design?

Yes. Send a drawing, a photo or a few words. We carve new blocks for it, print a sample, and match the full order to the one you approve.

I cannot sketch. Can you still help?

Yes. A photo, an old fabric or a few words is enough to begin.

Can you do stripes and checks?

Yes.

If we reorder, will the colour match?

Yes. When you reorder a print, we match it to the same sample you approved before.

Visiting and contact

Where is everything made?

Printing, stitching and finishing happen in our own units in Jaipur. You are welcome to visit, or to ask for photographs of any stage.

Who makes the prints?

Our artisans, women and men, belong to families who have printed fabric by hand for generations. Every block is carved by hand.

How do I reach you?

Write to info@poddarexp.com or use the contact page.
