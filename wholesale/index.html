How we work

Printed by hand, by families who know the work

Our artisans, women and men, belong to families who have printed fabric by hand for generations. Every block is carved by hand, and we carve new blocks for your idea.

Your colour, matched

We print a sample, you approve it, and we match the full order to it.

In our own units

Printing, stitching and finishing happen in our own units in Jaipur, so delivery is in our own hands. You are welcome to visit, or to ask for photographs of any stage.

Colour and handfeel

Colour fastness in washing is something we protect, and some of our buyers have our fabric tested at the best independent labs. We block print with a soft handfeel.

From the maker

Prices straight from the maker. We have sent our textiles to shops and brands in Europe, the USA and Australia for many years.
